dotss360 privacy policy

DOTSS360

Version 1.2.1 · Last updated: 19 October 2026

1. Who is responsible for your data

The controller of the data we collect through dotss360 is Francesc Arolas i Vall-llosera, an individual, with an address for notices at Carrer Pedreres 61, 17820 Banyoles (Girona), Spain. For any question about your data, you can write to franarolas@gmail.com.

2. What data we collect

Account data: your phone number, which we use to sign you in with a code sent by SMS. We do not use passwords.

Profile data: the name shown on leaderboards, an alias (the name the app greets you with), your preferred language, your daily step goal and, if you choose to give them, your date of birth, your gender and a profile photo. Date of birth, gender and photo are optional, and you can clear them at any time in the settings.

Step data: your step count for each day, broken down by hour, which we read from Apple Health or Google Health Connect only if you give us permission. We do not read the raw samples on your device or any other health data (no weight, height, heart rate or any other metric).

Participation data: the competitions and teams you join, your answers to competition quizzes (the answer, whether it was correct and when you answered) and any bonus steps awarded to you for quizzes or manually, together with the reason.

Technical data: an identifier for your device so we can send you notifications, pseudonymised app usage data and technical error reports (see section 6).

3. Why we process your data and on what legal basis

  • To create and manage your account and run the app: performance of the contract (the terms of use you accept when you sign up).
  • To read and use your step count to show you your progress and let you take part in competitions: your explicit consent, given in the informed consent and when you grant the Apple Health or Health Connect permission. You can withdraw it at any time in your device settings.
  • To send you notifications (milestones, competitions, quizzes): your consent, which you can withdraw at any time in the app settings.
  • If you give them to us, to use your date of birth and gender, together with your steps, to produce aggregated statistics about participants' physical activity in the future: your consent, which you can withdraw at any time by clearing this data in the settings. These statistics only show group totals and never identify you.
  • To improve the app and detect technical errors: our legitimate interest in offering a reliable service.

Your step data is never used for advertising, sold, or shared with third parties for commercial purposes.

4. Who can see your data

Within a competition. When you join a competition, anyone using the app who opens that competition can see on the leaderboard your name, your profile photo, your position, your total steps in that competition and any bonus steps you have earned and, in team competitions, your team and the team's total.

They can also open a summary card about you, which only contains information calculated from that data: how your position has changed since the previous day, your total steps split into walked steps and bonus steps, your average steps per day since you joined, the day you joined and, in team competitions, whether you are the captain and your share of the team's steps.

Participants of a competition (in team competitions, only members whose request has been approved) can also see who walked the most steps in that competition on the previous day, and that day's step count. Bonus steps do not count.

What nobody but you can see: your alias, your date of birth, your gender, your phone number, your daily step goal, your quiz answers and your day-by-day step history.

Our team. People with administrative permissions can access your data in a limited way and only when needed: administrators (technical support and running the service), the managers of each competition (only the participation data of the competitions they manage) and the team members in charge of data analysis (profile and step data, only for the statistics in section 3).

5. Who we share your data with

We use Google Firebase and Google Cloud (Google Ireland Limited and Google LLC) as processors to host the app: database, file storage, SMS sign-in, notifications, analytics and error reports. App data is stored in the European Union (europe-west1 region, Belgium). Some Google services may process data outside the EU, with the safeguards the law requires (the EU-US Data Privacy Framework and standard contractual clauses).

We do not share your date of birth or your gender with anyone. We do not sell your data or share it with third parties for advertising.

6. Analytics and error reports

We use Firebase Analytics with a pseudonymised identifier that is not your account's, and we never send your step count or any health data to it. We use Firebase Crashlytics to detect errors; reports carry an internal identifier (never your phone number or your name) so we can investigate the specific error.

7. How long we keep your data

We keep your data while your account is active. If you delete your account:

  • Your profile, photo, step count, quiz answers, awarded bonus steps and device identifiers are deleted.
  • Your participation in active or upcoming competitions is deleted completely.
  • Your participation in competitions that have already ended is kept to preserve historical leaderboards, but pseudonymised: your name and photo are removed and only an internal identifier remains, which is no longer linked to any account.

8. Your rights

You have the right to access, rectify and erase your data, to restrict or object to its processing, to data portability and to withdraw your consent at any time, without affecting processing carried out before. You can delete your account directly in the app (Settings, Delete account) or write to franarolas@gmail.com to exercise any of these rights; we will reply within one month. If you believe we have not handled your data properly, you can lodge a complaint with the Spanish Data Protection Agency (www.aepd.es).

9. Minors

You must be at least 14 years old to use dotss360. If you believe someone under 14 has created an account, write to us and we will delete it.

10. Security

We apply reasonable technical and organisational measures to protect your data, such as encrypted connections, account- and role-based access control, and team access limited to what is necessary.

11. Changes to this policy

If we make significant changes to this policy, we will tell you in the app and ask you to accept it again.
